About

From economics in Mazar-e-Sharif to entrepreneurship in Milan

I graduated with the highest GPA in the Department of National Economy at Balkh University, Afghanistan, where I also founded the Economics Faculty Research Club, mentored the faculty's British Parliamentary debate club and chaired the students' association. Those years taught me that research and community building go hand in hand.

In Italy I completed an M.Sc. in Global Entrepreneurship, Economics & Management at Università degli Studi dell'Insubria, with an Erasmus exchange in Innovation and Technology Management at the University of South-Eastern Norway. My thesis examined AI-driven security solutions in manufacturing through a case study of the steel industry.

Today I lead SheConnects, a social-impact startup connecting Afghan women with remote jobs, and I continue to publish on innovation management, AI adoption and development economics.

Research

Publications & research projects

Working papers and a thesis spanning AI in industry, cross-border knowledge transfer and development economics. All are available on ResearchGate.

  1. 2024
    Master thesis

    AI-Driven Security Solutions in Manufacturing: Case Study from Steel Industry

    How artificial intelligence can strengthen safety and security practices on the factory floor, grounded in a case study from the steel sector. Università degli Studi dell'Insubria, November 2024.

  2. 2024
    Preprint

    Knowledge Transfer in International Acquisitions: Challenge and Barriers

    An examination of the organisational and cultural obstacles that hinder knowledge transfer when firms acquire across borders. November 2024.

  3. 2023
    Preprint

    Artificial Intelligence Startups in Italy: Their Role in Decision-Making

    A study of the Italian AI startup landscape and how these young firms shape managerial decision-making. 2023.

  4. 2021
    Preprint

    HDI, GDP, Urban Population, and CO2 Emission in Afghanistan: A Multiple Regression Analysis

    A quantitative analysis of the relationship between human development, economic output, urbanisation and carbon emissions in Afghanistan. December 2021.
