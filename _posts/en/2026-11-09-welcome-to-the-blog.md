This blog is where I will share notes from my work at the intersection of entrepreneurship, innovation management and artificial intelligence.

<!--more-->

## What to expect

Three threads run through most of what I do, and they will run through this blog too:

1. **Social innovation and inclusive work.** Lessons from building [SheConnects](https://www.sheconnects.work/), a startup that helps Afghan women find remote jobs, and from the wider world of impact-driven ventures.
2. **AI adoption in organisations.** How companies, from steel plants to startups, actually put artificial intelligence to work, and what gets in the way.
3. **Development economics.** Data-driven reflections on growth, human development and sustainability, with a particular interest in Afghanistan and the wider region.

Posts will be short and practical. Where a topic deserves a longer treatment, I will link to the relevant paper or preprint.

## Two languages

I write in English, and I live and work in Italy, so some posts will also appear in Italian. When a translation exists you will find a link at the top of the article.

Thank you for reading. If something here sparks an idea, you can find my contact details on the [home page]({{ "/" | relative_url }}).
